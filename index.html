Canada Expands National Digital Archives Initiative to Preserve Global Journalism

OTTAWA — Library and Archives Canada has announced a comprehensive expansion of its digital press preservation network, aiming to safeguard access to independent reporting across English, French, and international news agencies.

The initiative provides public repositories with verified access pathways to leading domestic and global journalism networks, ensuring that historical, cultural, and sociopolitical archives remain available for academic research and public consultation.
